TeamCP Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

TeamCP develops and operates Android games. This policy covers TeamCP games in general and their Google Play Games integration. See the policy for each game for details about the information it handles.

1. Operator

Data controller: TeamCP

Inquiries: teamcp.channel@gmail.com

TeamCP does not operate its own game accounts or ask players to enter their name, email address, phone number, or payment information directly in its apps.

2. Our games

  • Cat Gomoku
  • Cat Detective
  • Slime Express (Korean)
  • DEAD FREQUENCY
  • CatRush 3 (Korean)

3. Information commonly processed

  • Device storage: Game progress, settings, rewards, and unlocks are kept in Android app-specific storage.
  • Google Play Games (optional): Google may process authentication information and some game progress for sign-in, player identification, leaderboards, and saves. Single-player play is available without connecting.
  • Advertising: Some games use Unity LevelPlay and Unity Ads for banners or optional rewarded ads. Ad providers may process device and advertising identifiers, approximate location, and ad events to deliver and measure ads and prevent abuse.

TeamCP does not sell personal information or collect gameplay records on its own analytics servers.

4. External services

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Delete Google Play Games data
  • Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy

External providers may retain information or process it overseas according to their own policies.

5. Retention, deletion, and rights

You can remove locally stored information by clearing Android app data or uninstalling the app. For Google Play Games and advertising information, the providers’ retention, deletion, and rights procedures apply. To request access, correction, or deletion, email us with the app name and the type of request.

6. Children

TeamCP games are designed for a general audience, with a store target age of 13 and above. Content age ratings do not determine whether someone can consent to personal data processing.

7. Changes

When the types of information we process or the services we use change, we update the effective date on this page and on each game’s policy.

8. Privacy officer

  • Privacy officer: TeamCP Privacy Officer (privacy team)
  • Department for privacy matters and complaints: TeamCP Privacy Office
  • Contact: Email teamcp.channel@gmail.com

You can contact us at the address above about privacy questions, complaints, or remedies, and TeamCP will respond and handle them without delay.
