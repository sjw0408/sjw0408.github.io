Cat Dungeon: 3-Min Rush Privacy Policy

Effective November 23, 2026

Cat Dungeon: 3-Min Rush can be played without registering for an app account. Both the Google Play and ONE store editions use ad services and store game progress in app-specific storage. Google Play Games sign-in, leaderboards, and achievement synchronization are available only in the Google Play edition.

1. Personal Information Controller and Scope

TeamCP (the “Developer”) applies this policy to the following two editions of the Android game Cat Dungeon: 3-Min Rush.

  • Google Play edition · com.teamcp.catrush3
  • ONE store edition · com.teamcp.catrush3 (same package name as the Google Play edition, different signature)
  • Contact · teamcp.channel@gmail.com

The two editions have the same package name but different signatures, so you cannot install both on the same device. Saved data does not sync automatically between the editions. The ONE store edition does not include the Google Play Games SDK or ONE store account or payment SDKs. The Developer operates no app accounts or game servers and does not directly request your name, contact details, email address, photos, microphone access, or precise GPS location.

2. Information We Process and How We Collect It

2.1 Game Progress and On-Device Storage

The app keeps game currencies such as fish and souls, unlocked cats, relics and permanent upgrades, dungeon progress and statistics, local achievements and missions, and language and sound settings in Android app-specific storage (Jetpack DataStore). It does not send this information to the Developer’s own servers. Android isolates access to app-specific storage. The app itself does not separately encrypt the data.

The app allows Android backup. Depending on your device and account backup settings, the operating system or backup provider may back up and restore app data. Uninstalling the app alone may leave backup copies in place.

2.2 Ad Services

Both editions use the Unity LevelPlay mediation SDK and the same LevelPlay app settings (the same ad configuration), which may connect to ironSource Ads. The app also includes the Unity Ads SDK and adapter. Actual ad delivery and display depend on ad readiness and provider responses.

Ad formats include banners at the bottom of the screen, interstitial ads after a set number of completed runs, and optional rewarded ads. The app can request banners while you navigate between screens. It can request interstitial ads even if you do not tap a reward button. You can choose to watch rewarded ads to double end-of-run rewards, revive once per run, get a free relic chest, and more. The app grants the reward only after it receives the completion signal. You can continue playing the base game if an ad is unavailable or fails.

Ad providers may process the following information to serve ads, control frequency, prevent abuse, compile statistics, and diagnose problems.

  • Advertising identifiers such as the Google advertising ID, subject to device settings and availability, and app and device identifiers
  • Device model, operating system and app versions, display and language information, network type, and IP address
  • Approximate location inferred from an IP address or similar information, and information about ad requests, impressions, interactions, completions, and errors

2.3 Google Play Games (Google Play Edition Only)

The Google Play edition initializes Google Play Games Services. Authentication status may be processed depending on Google and device account settings. If you use leaderboards or achievement synchronization, Google services may process data such as your public gamer name and profile, player ID, highest stage reached, and achievements earned. You can still play the base single-player game and access local records without signing in.

The ONE store edition does not include this SDK, sign-in, online leaderboards, or Google achievement synchronization. Local achievements and records stay on the device.

3. Purposes of Use

  • Saving and restoring game progress and settings, and providing local records, achievements, and missions
  • Serving and measuring ads, granting rewards, controlling frequency, and preventing abuse
  • Integrating Play Games leaderboards and achievements in the Google Play edition, and diagnosing errors

5. Retention and Deletion

To clear local data on your device, go to Android Settings → Apps → Cat Dungeon: 3-Min Rush → Storage → Clear data. Uninstalling the app removes its app-specific storage. Operating system backups may still exist or be restored when you reinstall the app, so check your device or account backup settings. Data for the Google Play and ONE store editions must be managed separately.

The retention and deletion of ad-related data are subject to the policies of providers such as Unity and ironSource. You can manage Google Play Games data in your Google Account’s connection settings or through “Delete Play Games account & data” in the Play Games app. The ONE store edition does not send new records to Google Play Games.

6. Your Choices and Controls

  • Watching rewarded ads is optional, and you can play the base game without doing so. The app may request banner or interstitial ads without a separate choice to watch them.
  • Android ad settings offer controls such as deleting or resetting the advertising ID. Setting names may vary by operating system and device.
  • You can manage your Play Games connection and profile visibility for the Google Play edition in your Google Account and Play Games settings.
  • For privacy inquiries, including requests for access to or deletion of personal information, email teamcp.channel@gmail.com.

7. Children’s Privacy

8. Safeguards

Saved game data uses the access controls of Android app-specific storage. See each provider’s policy for details on how external SDKs process and protect information.

Additional Notice Under the Personal Information Protection Act (Added November 23, 2026)

Under Article 30 of the Personal Information Protection Act and Article 31 of its Enforcement Decree, this notice explains users’ rights and how to exercise them, how the policy applies to children under 14 and their legal representatives, the safeguards in place, and who serves as the Privacy Officer. It applies together with the rest of this policy.

Data Subject Rights and How to Exercise Them

  • You may at any time request access to, correction or deletion of, or suspension of the processing of your personal information that TeamCP processes. You may also withdraw consent (Articles 35 to 37 of the Personal Information Protection Act).
  • To submit a request, email the contact below (teamcp.channel@gmail.com) with the app name and details of your request. Since the app does not have accounts, include information that can help TeamCP locate the records, such as the approximate date you submitted a bug report or its reference number. A legal representative or authorized agent may also submit a request. In that case, TeamCP may ask for proof of their authority to act for you.
  • TeamCP will tell you the outcome within 10 days of receiving the request. If the law allows TeamCP to restrict or deny the request, or if TeamCP cannot handle it within that period, it will explain why and tell you how to object.
  • TeamCP does not retain information stored only on your device, such as app records and settings. You can delete it yourself using the app’s deletion feature, clearing app storage in Android Settings, or uninstalling the app.
  • For information handled by external providers such as ad providers or Google, you can also submit requests using the providers’ contact details in the overseas transfer notice above.
  • If your rights or interests have been infringed, you can request dispute mediation or advice from the Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee (1833-6972, www.kopico.go.kr), the Personal Information Infringement Report Center (118, privacy.kisa.or.kr), the Supreme Prosecutors’ Office (1301, www.spo.go.kr), or the Korean National Police Agency (182, ecrm.police.go.kr).

Children Under 14 and Their Legal Representatives

  • Korea’s Personal Information Protection Act requires consent from a legal representative when consent is the basis for processing personal information of a child under 14 (Article 22-2).
  • The app does not ask for a date of birth or age and has no features to verify age or the consent of a legal representative. The app processes information the same way regardless of age, so the same processing described in this policy applies to users under 14. The app sends ad services an app-level “not directed to children” signal. This signal does not reflect age verification for individual users.
  • You can control personalized ads through Android’s ad privacy and advertising ID settings. Users under 14 should work with a parent or guardian to delete the advertising ID in Android Settings so that it cannot be used for personalized ads.
  • If you are under 14, please leave personal information such as your name or contact details out of bug reports and inquiries.
  • A legal representative may request access to, correction or deletion of, or suspension of the processing of a child’s personal information, and may withdraw consent. The process and response period are the same as those described above under “Data Subject Rights and How to Exercise Them.” TeamCP requests only the minimum information needed to verify guardianship and deletes it after verification.
  • If TeamCP learns that a child under 14’s personal information was processed without the consent of their legal representative, TeamCP will promptly delete any of that information it holds.

Safeguards for Personal Information

  • Android keeps app records and settings in storage isolated for each app. The app sends this information to TeamCP servers only in the cases described in this policy.
  • The app encrypts information it sends over the internet, including ad requests and Google service integration data, using HTTPS/TLS.
  • TeamCP minimizes the personal information it holds by offering no registration and collecting no passwords, names, or phone numbers.
  • TeamCP has no servers that receive game records. Information on the device stays there until you delete it.
  • TeamCP keeps inquiry emails no longer than necessary after responding, then deletes them. TeamCP retains personal information only in electronic files and deletes them at the end of the retention period.
  • The safeguards for information handled by external providers such as ad providers or Google are governed by those providers’ policies.
